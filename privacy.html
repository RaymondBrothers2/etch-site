Privacy Policy

Effective: July 5, 2026 · TAB (Time · Air · Breath)

The short version

ETCH is a private, permanent record of your life. Your record belongs to you. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use engagement tricks. You are the customer, never the product.

What we collect

Account: your email address, name, and birthdate (used to compute your life timeline).
Your content: the moments you choose to capture — photos, videos, voice recordings, and text — stored so they can be preserved permanently, which is the purpose of the app.
Device permissions: camera, microphone, and photo library are used only to capture or import moments you initiate. Face ID is used only to lock the app on your device — biometric data never leaves your device.

Where it lives

Your record is stored with our cloud database provider (Supabase) using industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Access is restricted to your authenticated account.

AI features

Optional features (pencil sketches, factual daily summaries) send only the content you choose to our AI service providers, solely to produce that result. AI output is always labeled. Our AI never interprets your life, scores you, or writes as you.

What we never do

We never sell your data. We never show advertising. We never share your private record with third parties except the service providers needed to run the app. No public follower counts, likes, or engagement metrics.

Sharing you control

Everything is private by default. Content becomes visible to others only when you explicitly share it, and public release is revocable while you live.

Permanence and deletion

ETCH is designed as a permanent record: sealed moments cannot be edited. You may delete your account, which removes your data from our production systems; contact us at the address below.

Children

ETCH is not directed at children under 13. Minors' content is private and never publicly visible.
